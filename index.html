    Kapeng Kabado

    A coffee shop made for students, overthinkers, crammers, night owls, and everyone trying to survive one cup at a time.

    Kapeng Kabado started from a simple idea: coffee is not just a drink. For many Filipinos, it becomes part of late-night study sessions, unfinished deadlines, awkward situationships, sudden life realizations, and random 3AM breakdowns.

    Our café serves quality coffee with a touch of Filipino humor and Gen Z energy. Every drink is inspired by moments we all know too well.

    At Kapeng Kabado, we believe coffee tastes better when shared with friends, laughter, and a little bit of drama. There is always a place for you here.

    So take a sip, breathe a little, and remember:

    "Gising ka na nga, kabado ka pa."
