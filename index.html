Our Story

Get To Know
Kapeng Kabado

A coffee shop made for students, overthinkers, crammers, night owls, and everyone trying to survive one cup at a time.

Kapeng Kabado started from a simple idea: coffee is not just a drink. For many Filipinos, it becomes part of late-night study sessions, unfinished deadlines, awkward situationships, sudden life realizations, and random 3AM breakdowns. We wanted to create a brand that feels real, funny, comforting, and relatable.

Our café serves quality coffee with a touch of Filipino humor and Gen Z energy. Every drink is inspired by moments we all know too well — from thesis panic to waiting for a reply that never comes. Behind every cup is the goal to make people smile, relax, and feel less alone in their daily chaos.

At Kapeng Kabado, we believe coffee tastes better when shared with friends, laughter, and a little bit of drama. Whether you are reviewing for exams, working on your dreams, or simply escaping reality for a while, there is always a place for you here.

So take a sip, breathe a little, and remember:

"Gising ka na nga, kabado ka pa."
